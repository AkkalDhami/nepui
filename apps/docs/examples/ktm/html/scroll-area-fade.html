Vernacular architecture is building done outside any academic tradition, and without professional guidance.

It is not a particular architectural movement or style, but rather a broad category of building types and methods of construction.

This type of architecture usually serves immediate, local needs and is constrained by the materials available in its region.

It reflects local traditions and cultural practices, and the skills of builders who were rarely given any attribution.

More recently, it has been examined for energy-conscious design and construction.
