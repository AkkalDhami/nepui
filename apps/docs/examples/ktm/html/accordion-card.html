Subscription & Billing
Common questions about your account, plans, payments and cancellations.
What subscription plans do you offer?
We offer three subscription tiers: Starter ($9/month), Professional ($29/month), and Enterprise ($99/month). Each plan includes increasing storage limits, API access, priority support, and team collaboration features.
How does billing work?
Plans are billed at the start of each cycle, monthly or annually. You can switch plans at any time.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Billing settings and choose Cancel plan. Your access continues until the end of the current billing cycle.
