Data attributes

Align

data-align start, center or end.

Aligned to start

Its left edge lines up with the trigger.

Aligned to center

It is centered on the trigger.

Aligned to end

Its right edge lines up with the trigger.

Side

data-side top, right, bottom or left. It flips when there is no room.

Top

Opens above the trigger.

Right

Opens to the right.

Bottom

Opens below the trigger.

Left

Opens to the left.
